# Lies to a Consumer

*Published: 2023-10-10*

---

As the wonder boy of Zerodha, Nikhil Kamath sweeps the internet with his podcasts, interviews and other intellectual debates on morality, altruism and how trading is like dating, I wanted to pause and reflect on my journey since last few months.  

Kishore Biyani of Big Bazaar said that all brands are made on and thrive on three principles - Greed, Fear and Altruism. I wanted to pick this idea and take it far to say that this is how all systems operate on, subtracting the altruism and adding Identity to the mix. Greed to do/get better than what you did/got yesterday. Fear of not getting the same as what other people are getting or what you got yesterday. Identity that you can wear to fit within a larger community who share the same identity so that you don't feel alone. This is how religion works, this is how large corporations get away by paying peanuts to employees, this is why quiet luxury exists and this is why companies literally make you wear t-shirts with their logo on them. This is also why the famous or infamous Andrew Tate bought the Bugatti. Humans carry a heavy weight of existentialism individually. They need a medium to float in. Identity becomes that common medium for all of us.  

Separately, today also marks a quarter of being a non-consumer of Instagram. And it feels like such a good riddance. I am not in a constant state of fomo and feeling small about myself. I consumed more structure knowledge than ever in these last 3 months. Business, economy, stock market, politics, world history, psychology and what not. Anything I have consumed online has been very mindful, by default. I watched some of the great movies - Mind walk, Phantom tread, Closer, Indecent proposal etc. I started doing active searches on what I wanted to watch based on my emotional state instead of saving reels from every new "cinephile". For  mindless watching I went through the Hangover, Ironman series and BBT.  I stepped out of house more and watched a bunch of theatre and movies. The reason I want to form these habits subconsciously is because the people in power benefits us from doing opposite. I sometimes think Instagram targets  a bunch of sufficiency deprived people by creating meaning out of trending patterns and lingos and trends that are non applicable to any part of life beyond that the local theatrics. Content has always been a way to brainwash people. With digital revolution, this weapon has only gotten stronger. The day we stop making a choice on what we consume, we also stop being free. And the worst part is, some of us have already forgotten that there was ever a choice.
