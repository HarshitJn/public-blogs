# My take on AI

*Published: 2025-04-27*

---

For a change, I wanted to play oracle. Not because I have a crystal ball, but because it’ll be fun to look back in a year and see how hilariously wrong (or weirdly right) I was. So here it is: a note to myself, a silly little time capsule, trying to make sense of where the world might be heading.
Cash Is King (Again)Markets might wobble. Stocks could dip temporarily. Gold may go full rocket ship. Having a decent pile of cash might feel boring, but it could turn out to be the most exciting move. In times of chaos, liquidity = freedom.
Bye Bye coasting culture
Companies might shrink head counts, not because they’re failing, but because tech makes fewer people do more. Just “doing your job” may not cut it anymore. Outperforming your team? That’s the new job security.
Sorry, coasting culture.
Tech Will 10x
New apps for every problem. AI tools spawning like mushrooms after rain.
What took 5 years to build will take 1. Faster, cheaper, better. Technology won’t vanish. It’ll multiply. It’ll be like air: invisible, everywhere, necessary. We won’t stop using tech. We’ll stop noticing we’re using it. We might all ditch smartphone and be wearing a smart glass all the time. I remember watching this when I was 12 years old but GTA looked way more real back then. How the turn tables.
Offline Work Will Become Sexy Again
Ironically, as tech dominates, the real world might shine more. You can’t outsource restaurant service. You can’t AI a vitamin D tablet. AI can paint a canvas but maybe it wont appeal as much. Healthcare, hospitality, law, commodity businesses, art, local logistics, they’ll matter more. When everyone’s coding, maybe making a good cup of coffee becomes premium.
Content Explosion
Creating any content - blogs, youtube motivational bullshit, reels, memes, podcast, advertisement - has become manifolds easier. Everyone can showcase anything. It will become even more important to consume mindfully.
Conclusion
The next wave isn’t about knowing everything. It’s about knowing what not to pay attention to. Keep cash. Stay sharp. Avoid the narratives. Bet on effort. As with every change, it often can seem intimidating but only those who embrace it completely, stand to benefit out of it. And future me, if you’re reading this a year from now, I hope you’re smiling at how dumb this sounds or quietly proud of how spot-on it was. And hopefully, GOOG wouldnt still be trading at 150$.
Either way, we tried. And thats everything.
