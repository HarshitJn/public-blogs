# God?

*Published: 2024-07-14*

---

Having been agnostic for a large number of years, ever since the theory of evolution and its precise presentation by Yuval Noah Harari, I think I found the answer to Why God?
A mountain sized ego, a present from the book of logic made me an atheist. Logic seemed to be the correct answer to everything. This soon changed when I ventured into the world of art. The absurdity of the beauty in the world made me think that there has to be something more than what can de deduced by something as stiff as logic. 
Not that I am a core believer now. But I sort of have an understanding of it which is more light weight than refuting or getting attached to any side. This stems from the observation that God is a provider of 2 very important pillars to human existence: hope and peace. It provides the much needed slowness which is seemingly impossible to find in an otherwise fast routine the 21st century has rendered us all with. And then there is so much dogma in the world that one cannot escape from the right and the wrong. In this multitude of conflict, one finds oneself always in doubt. As if he or she was not good enough for the world. And in so, having that anchor gives an all encompassing goodness about oneself. There is no good or bad when you are in a temple and hence you feel as if a weight has been lifted from you, atleast I do. 
Attachment leads to suffering, so does negative attachment. Running away from something is attaching yourself to the opposite belief which is no good either. Life is lived fully when there is self love, which can grow only in the absence of self conflict.
