# Scribblings

*Published: 2024-08-11*

---

Idol worship, chanting, meditation, yoga and all the other first order principles take you to a single thing - destroying your ego. Doing that eradicates your fear about yourself and you're snapped back into the moment. This is when you start living the life truly.  

Most of the self development might just be about working your way through the 7 deadly sins - pride, greed, wrath, envy, lust, gluttony and sloth. Pride and Wrath are direct byproducts are ego. Gluttony and Lust are just obsessions, attachments taken too far. Attachment stems from ego. Envy is self-insufficiency, a form a fear projected onto others. Fear stems from ego. Sloth is for kids. So again it all just bottles down to working with your ego, its greed and obsessions being the tricky ones.  

"Ek bagal me chand hoga, Ek bagal me rotiyan" - The duality of hustle and rest potrayed beautifully. Life possibly will just be an endless discovery towards answering this. I hope I can find an answer early enough to enjoy the rest of it without a perpetual dissonance.  

Markets are all about narrative. Create a story, step by step, make assumptions but dont reveal them, establish possibilities as facts and lure people to follow your narrative by talking smooth. Most people would avoid the friction of going against a well presented narrative and believe whatever is sold in the end of the story. Seller needs to make sure that the audience buys the story, buying the product will become a side effect. This applies to businesses, political manifestos, corporate or transactional relationships and all other forms of markets.  

Science became everything because people started misusing trust. In the absence of trust, a man resorts to logic and evidence.  

Once you start living meditatively, you start to see the truth. Then you dont need any more rules to live by. You become a self-aware self-reconciling self-correcting machine (:P) with a perpetual state of satisfaction in everything that comes by. You become gratuitous of all you have and all there is. All your shortcomings stop becoming your worries but rather you casually start working on them if you feel like.  

If there is no memory, there cannot be rights or wrongs and henceforth no fear exists in a stateless conscious experience.  

The media has blurred rights and wrongs and somehow I fell right into to it too.  

God gives hope but also a medium and a reminder to enjoy slow things in life.  

The darker the subject you more mastery you need to have to make light of it. And making light of it is not belittling the problem but instead totally accepting it and living through it easily without a constant fear about the mention of the topic.  

It takes infinitely more time to respond to something stupid said in a whim.  

Aren't we all like threads in a fabric, laying parallel next to each other at first sight but supported by 1000 other threads when looked deeeper.  

Nothing is more rewarding as an artist than the infinite wait of the hour where a complete inner clarity is developed after relentless cognitive dissonance and innumerable experiments with the self.  

The thought is weighed down by the old prejudice of right and wrong, and then on the other hand crippled by modern virtue signalling. What you’re left with is the fraction of your original thought. Rest of it is in deep muddy waters. It requires deep courage and extreme clarity to build up on that original thought, live with it, both in its darkness and light, until all of the mud is washed away and the idea is not just a thought now, it becomes a living part of you.  

People who are hunrgy and people who have everything are the only ones who are not satiated.  

Every life must have a place where it can find solitude which lets it wander away from all the non stop chatter. This is the only way something everlasting can be felt. A feeling where intelligence takes a step back and can barely process the expansivity of the emotions thus felt. A feeling which is necessary for each life to be sustained. Places like these are inconsequential to the eyes of a mathematician but often in these places are found the answers to questions which are themselves quite unsayable and unstateable.  

Nothing worth having comes easy.  

Perennially confused if it’s my sadness of not finding myself or the happiness of still receiving love which is more opaque.  

Throughout the life one must go through the discovery of feelings, happy or sad, which are dauntingly opaque at first, slowly transcending into translucent before you truly see them and then become one with them.  

Everything dies with time. Other than the love which is felt deeply. It only needs a free mind to feel it repeatedly whenever needed.  

Our greatest misunderstanding is that we look to measure our lives on the countable. How can you ever be satisfied when there is a larger counter available. The secret therefore was always to measure life on the uncountables like love, peace, awareness and truth.  

We will be remembered as the generation who had everything and made that a reason for its downfall.  

Every year i find myself confused more than before and i realise that its just more ego shedding away.
