# I'm here.

*Published: 2023-01-08*

---

Im here. This is enough. There's so much happening here.
This is both my feeling right now as I write this first blog and how I choose to live each moment of my life. There's never nothing happening. There are no ordinary moments.
As a software person I have been typing on my keyboard for long. But this feels different. There's no ulterior motive to do this. There's no one telling me to do this. There's no reward. There's no validation. I plan on keeping this anonymous . Im happy Wordpress doesn't have a like button that Medium has. As I press these keys, I wonder why would humans prefer to put an effort to do something hard with no gains over just sleep around. 
See, for the longest time, I've lived my life chasing the medals hung all over the society's pedestal. I accepted all the dogma hoardings put around, until one day a question struck me - How will you measure your life? I pondered over it for long enough until I got distracted by the usual bustle of life and moved on. Until years later, I was forced to reconsider the question. More on this later.
Music plays in the background. My Ikea lamp lights up the peach walls. I sip the golden milk (Trust me, Its the best, I'll put a recipe towards the end) at this golden hour. I write these white words on a black board. I am zoned out of the world. I have been wanting this for so long. Something so simple. A place to write my thoughts. A place to capture what I feel. Somewhere to capture my existence. That I lived. Isn't that all we want. To be seen? 
Maybe thats what it has all been ever about. Finding beautiful things and doing them for the hell of it. I think its Bukowski who said "Find what you love and let it kill you". Not saying I want to be the best blogger alive but that if I die dancing in this small life I created for myself with all the beautiful things around, would I be happy? I don't know for sure but I find it really hard to say no.
I'm not going to bore you with how this was supposed to be a daily blog since 1st January and how life came in between. We all know better. Chaos is where the magic happens. No good ever comes in fighting it.
One last thing before I go. Something to remind me when I forget why I started this: Where else would we be found, where else would we be born, if not for the memories?
PS: Recipe for Golden milk
Take 11/2 glass of no-fat milk. Put half inch ginger, 1 teaspoon turmeric, 5-6 cardamom, 5-6 cloves, 1 tea spoon crushed black pepper and a piece of cinnamon stick. Get the milk to boil and leave it on medium-low flame for about 10 minutes.  Serve hot. Best had before bed time.
