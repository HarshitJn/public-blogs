# Inflection

*Published: 2024-05-04*

---

Social media, on the go internet access on the phone, dark patterns and an overwhelming wave of fomo is unprecedented. Our mind cannot help but give in. We all live in an endless state of running towards something. And if you try hard, you will resolve to just stay away from all of it and go back to living life the old way. Without phones. Without internet. If you try really hard, you might be able to do so as well. I did it for 6 months. But the catch is this: running away from something is equally catastrophic as running towards something. 
The real way out is to strike a balance between having fun, embracing the chaos and living a slow and controlled life.
Recently, for the last 6 odd months, I let myself go. I left all the control and embraced the chaos fully. I had a blast. But like all good things, it got boring after a while and so I decided to return to my older system of productivity and peace. But I realised that one cannot force focus onto oneself. You simply cannot say that I am going to shut off all distractions from today and just be productive. The problem is we make decisions through logic and forget that we operate on emotions. And nothing in the world is stiffer than logic. The irony is that the more you plan to be focused and productive, the more you will find yourself annoyed with every little task that comes your way. It is only through the gaps of a fun filled schedule that our mind and body is able to perform all those mundane tasks.
