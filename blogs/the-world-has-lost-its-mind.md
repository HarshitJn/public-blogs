# The world has lost its mind

*Published: 2023-01-15*

---

Don't get me wrong, I know the phrase is used generally to refer to someone when they do or say something absolutely crazy which you don't find fitting to your model of world. But I use it here quite literally. I think we all have lost our minds. Relentless news feeds, social media flame wars, infinite notifications, 100s of emails to catch upto, relationship squabbles and an endless dissatisfaction with life, despite of doing it all. Maybe thats the issue, doing it all. We have all gotten too distant from the meaning of why we are here that it is difficult to snap out of this rut and just be here. To quote Tyler Durden, "We’re the middle children of history. No purpose or place. We have no Great War. No Great Depression. Our Great War is a spiritual war, our Great Depression is our lives." 
The problem is we all know this fact. But the happiness industry has made happiness and satisfaction far too elusive . With the rise of wokeism, toxic positivity,  positive psychology and dismissing negative emotions, we have got ourselves bewildered. Instagram trends are the new gods. Or the people behind those algorithms. We have chosen to associate our views with those selected few in power. It gives an easy way out. You have an easy identity because you follow so and so, now you don't need to work through understanding the intricacies of the subject. Every few weeks a new "ism" floats the internet and we find ourselves forced to choose sides. Therein lies a larger problem with the internet. With the immediate and infinite reach, everyone gets to know everything. We are bombarded with information that we cant process. We are burdened with half baked knowledge we cant use. And amidst all this, we have chosen to let our opinions be shaped by what we see. And what is identity if not a collection of opinions. You take a man, re-wire all his opinions to match yours, because yours trend well on twitter and congratulations you've reduced the human population by 1. Our unique way of thinking is what defines us but it's almost as if having a different opinion about something makes you a smaller person. Or worse yet, having no opinion about something is considered a crime.
There is a dire need to relax the mind. It's time we start talking about the Mind Gyms. Train our mind to focus amidst the chaos. To be more observant about the impulses. To be more mindful. Mindful relaxation. We need the ability to snap out of a heated debate or a meeting at office or when you are bothered by too much traffic. See where it fits in the larger perspective and where you fit in the world. Rather than feeling belittled by the expansiveness of the world, we should get encouraged to see that there's so much more to do. The world is so vast that anything we do is insignificant. But instead of being bummed down by it, we could let it empower us to be free. Free of results. Free of expectations. To do it as a play thing. As Alan Watts says, "Man suffers only because he takes seriously what the gods made for fun". Or let me just quote the OG here, good old Buddha. Attachment is the root of all suffering. We are so badly attached to our identity, what we do, the outcomes of our actions, that we've forgotten the joy that is in doing things, while they are happening. Few years back I wrote,
This thing you call future
Is only a ricochet from your past,
The present that you know
Is merely a notion of an execution window,
The train’s already moving
And life’s already happening
And you are busy defining the window?
All the meaning we associate to our actions inhibits us to actually come to terms with its purest form. The action itself. That is the only meaning. Doing because it has to be done. That is the only truth. There is no larger why. And that is why Nihilism and Buddhism are so closely related. I often feel the full and the empty are more similar to each other than anything in between. The villain and the superhero always have a large set of commonalities.
Recently I was watching the movie, "Memoirs Of A Geisha" and it inspired me to write something. I'll leave you with it:I dance. I sing. It is not for me to want. It is for me to only climb because the plains are on the move and falling in cracks in not an option. It is for me to watch the sun set and wait for it to rise. It is for me to hope it will. Is is for me to watch the reflection of the fire in my eyes and watch it burn me.
It is for me to look at the leaves rising when all else falls around relentlessly. It is for me to incessantly run. At the same place. It is for me to float in the water when the flood comes and everything fallen is swept away. It is for me to hope that that leaf still remains. It is for me to find that leaf. It is not for me to delve into the secrets of universe.
It is not for me to ask why.
