# The Beauty of

*Published: 2024-02-26*

---

Ive been away from writing for quite some time now. I took a new resolution to write something each day and I honoured it for 300 days straight. At that point it became too much for me to keep track on all my emotions and activities. I wanted to let life slip away and for me to enjoy the slope for a while. And so this blog entry is a non cohesive one. It talks about some of the things I felt deeply during this time.
For the longest time I have strived for perfection. I have found peace only at 1 or at absolute 0. It helped to think that since I haven’t even started that one thing at all, it is better than starting and leaving it in the middle of nowhere. Life lately has taught me otherwise. There’s so much beauty in the halves. Watch that cricket match from the middle of nowhere and enjoy it to the fullest. Have few days where you work a lot and then few where you do absolutely bare minimum. Operate in a sine curve. Try for a healthy lifestyle but keep it lax. Enjoy those little moments where someone is late and you’re left to do nothing. Obsessions are for robots. Extremes are fun but only when they are balanced out with averages.
In other news, I have found great solace in watching Irfan Khan act. Life of Pi, Piku, Lunchbox or the lesser known Rog. Of course his body language and dialogue delivery is perfect but I really loved the dialogues themselves. You can feel that he has a sense of still in his head and that manifests in all dimensions of his acting. It’s almost like he is not acting but is really just reacting to life thats coming his way. More and more I’ve understood life, more and more I’ve started to become like him. We’re in a sea of madness, the least we can do is be at ease.
This understanding stemmed from another reflection I had. We dont chase hard things in an obsession to have them. We do it because it takes us closer to ourselves. It connects us to our own energy and unites us with the world through that synchronous rhythm. It takes us to a blissful place where the wrong and the right has no meaning. What prevails is our purest, boundless selves.
In the world of computer and algorithms, it is said that a system can only hold 2 of 3 between Consistency, Availability and Performance. You cannot make a system that is super fast, always working and always correct. I think it applies to lot more places. Between Health, Fun and work, you can only manage 2. Between living in the moment, chasing power and an innocent indulgence, you can only 2 lest you should be pushed to constant state of cognitive dissonance. Once you understand this, you make your peace with have just 2/3. Thats good enough. Mediocrity is underrated and hustle culture is here to take our breaths away. I want to be cognisant of which mode I operate in for which part of my life.
Separately, something that Ive thought the most about in these last 4 months is how much full is similar to empty. My reaction towards things and situations often makes people believe Im unaware and unable to respond. At work or otherwise. In a meeting of 12 people when everyone queues their hands to speak a point which is dumber than the dumbest person in the room, I chose to not speak at all. Save that energy. This has costed me a great deal however. Ive learnt that people when not sure, would just assume the worst about you. Nobody will really look into the depths of you to understand what you are. They will really judge you based on how you come across as, your speech, your words and your body language. It’s only fair. This century is short on time. Only a few people will go beyond words to understand you deeply. In a world preaching about “show not tell”, I forgot that this narrative itself is told more than it is showed. It’s a oxymoron in that sense. The world operates on impressions and you gotta put it out there for the world.
