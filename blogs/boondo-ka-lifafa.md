# Boondo ka lifafa

*Published: 2024-12-22*

---

Khafa tha samandar se
Toh ek chithi likhi
Ek nazm aur kuch sangeeda shabd
Ek nadi ke tat pe jaake
Bharose se use saunp diya
Samandar se khrodit tha na mai
Lifafe me samete hue shabd
Kuch der nadi ke saath
khilkhilate hue behte gaye
Na jaane kyun nadi apne astitv ko samundar me baha deti hai
Kitne vipreet hain ye
Maaf karein mai thoda bhatak gaya
Meri Chitthi samandar tak pohonchi
Aur mere shabdo ki syaahi failne lagi
Ek ek karke saare shabd doob gaye
Mai samjha ki yahi reet hogi
Samandar me doobke hi use samjha ja skta hoga
Shayad samandar meri chitthi ka jawab dega.
Kayi dino se barsaat nhi hui
Abki baar boondo ka maane samjhunga
