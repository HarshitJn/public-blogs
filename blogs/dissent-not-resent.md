# Dissent, not Resent

*Published: 2023-09-22*

---

Something got me pondering today. On the way back home, I was having a conversation with a colleague in the office cab. I was mindfully respectful and the conversation seemed to be going good until we were talking about the work. As soon as it shifted to life outside office, I noticed a couple of not so nice remarks nonchalantly landing my way. They were presumptuous and belittling.  I was taken aback but quickly brushed them away and continued the conversation in a polite way. Only that I had lost face and most likely the person was not aware of this. At least I want to give the benefit of doubt and believe that it was not intentional. And I think this has happened to all of us. Being taken advantage of because you were too polite, too nice. And what do we do, we become resentful and rude to everyone. And therein lies the problem. We become the same person. We start putting others down to feel better. Is this the best way out, I ask. I think a smaller, more progressive fix would be to be polite but when someone disrespects your boundaries, express your dissent, then and there. And it does not have to be a full fledged moral policing. If the remark was nonchalant, your dissent could be casual too. Just enough for them to know that you were not pleased by it. And if this is done politely, they will end up respecting you more for standing up for yourself. And once we build this habit, we stop holding grudges against people. It liberates us to have more open conversations with the world. It improves the quality of almost all relationships and they start to flourish.
