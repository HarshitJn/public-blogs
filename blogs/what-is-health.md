# What is Health?

*Published: Undated*

---

Following the happiness industry, there has been a sudden rise in the Calorie industry. Suddenly there is so much chatter about the protein intake, the calories burnt through the day. Health has seemingly just become about the fat percentage. Ever think if thats all? If you think you would see the drivers of this movement – the massive nutrition supplement and protein brands, the dairy industry, the ‘healthy’ snack industry, smart watch companies, gyms and the nutrition trackers. Its a huge fucking PR.  

Why do we forget about the blood flow, organ health, stability, flexibility, tendon health, pain management, long hours of sitting, bad sleep postures? What about hours spent on screen trying to sedate yourself? Because its convenient to buy yourself the kick of fitness through a protein shake and an hour at gym while rotting away the entire day in a health worsening lifestyle.  

Real thing is taking care of your body – it can be through lifting weights but it has to be supported by everything else around it. But it can also be so much simpler than that – doing house chores? keep walking a bit in the house? play with a toddler? take a stroll in the park? little bit of yoga? lie flat on the floor?
