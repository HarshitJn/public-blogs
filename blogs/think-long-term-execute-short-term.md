# Think long term, execute short term

*Published: 2023-10-11*

---

Nothing changes day by day and when you look back a decade, everything has changed. We all underestimate the things we can do in a single day and overestimate what we can achieve in a year. I think this one stuck with me deeply. I have observed myself to envision my years/decades and let the days and weeks be on auto-pilot. And I think this has to do with having very clear answers to Why of it all. Instead of focussing on defining goals of each little aspect of your life, when you have the ultimate light you want to follow, it guides you inadvertently at each step you take. Take for instance, if you know you want to live a pain-free, sickness free life with high physical and mental functioning, you don't need to have an artificial goal on going to the gym X days out of Y. You don't need to force yourself to manage your diet. Sleeping on time doesn't become a task. When you have that principle imbibed within, your subconscious guides you to do them automatically.  

Plan for years execute in hours. Think long term. And reflect but don't criticise too much too often. Give yourself the freedom to fail in short term so that you can automatically course correct and fix the long term. Too many of us perfectionists try to fixate on short term perfection that it limits us in achieving long term sustainable objective growth. You don't want to become a puppet constricted by its own strings which seldom take you forward and more often than not make you get entangled in your own mess. You want to be able to play a fool, risk being wrong, and eventually since you are not bound within these goal shaped chains, you'll end up getting to your destination. Thats how stock markets work, it goes up and down and then sideways and what not. It's stumbles all across the place, but in the long term it eventually delivers. Imagine reacting to each single movement of the market and adjusting your portfolio.  

This year has taught me many things in different planes and perseverance is the common theme across. Interestingly this was my new year resolution as well. To be more patient. It's uncanny that life presented me with exactly fitting opportunities. Or maybe I sensed I was entering a life where patience would be what would differentiate me from my past. Tough times in the job market, injuries during workouts, issues with the colleagues at work, expectation mismatch with family and friends, managing long bouts of boredom. In most of these situations, I became restless and tried to get to the solution real quick. Almost artificially. I will do this and the next day everything should be alright. Of course that doesn't work. What came out of these was an ability to remain calm, look at things objectively, manage the emotions around them, find the right action and work hard in silence. I became more independent than ever and needed much lesser venting, if any. Acceptance came easy and enabled action. There is still some distance to go into looking at things objectively and managing emotions but I guess there is  unknown known somewhere which will come out organically. If it were a known unknown I would be able to solve it more structurally.  

PS: Thought for the day: Psychology has answers to all hows. Philosophy is where the answers of all whys lie in. Religion is what fills the void of identity. Politics is what leverages the manifestations of different form of identity to drive masses. Business is the mistress of politics. Money and power are the only drivers of the world. Altruism is the packaging that makes all of it co-exist with the beauty of the world.
