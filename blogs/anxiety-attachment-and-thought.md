# Anxiety, Attachment & Thought

*Published: 2024-07-05*

---

Last 2 months were heavy and they put me back pre-2020 times when anxiety used to knock frequently on my door and I was busy thinking all the time. After a lot of tussle I was able to break out of it, later than I expected myself to, but then again sooner than it could have taken really.  

And so this is what I understood about anxiety, worry or all the negative emotions. I could find 3 ways to think about it:  

Scientifically: Evolutionarily our brain is wired to think about possible negative futures because it helps prepare our brain to avoid them by taking necessary action. Eating junk in the long run will come to bite you, so there is a associated guilt which stops you from doing that. However, the same worry becomes an anti-pattern when that's all you focus on. If, as a human who has only limited things in control, take the necessary actions to circumvent that negative future, you dont really need to be sad or suffer from that future worry. If you keep thinking about it, you will not be able to come out of that thought loop. This will prevent you from taking the right action because all the energy is just spend on thinking and your mind and body is drained already. This is the reason most overthinkers are also escapists. They think too much that all they are left with is just running away from the problem. It is best if one just does what is needed and be happy about doing it.  

Spiritually: Lot of text is written about how Attachment leads to Suffering. Essentially its saying the same thing. Attachment to something happens when you fragment the world into two halves - good and bad. You then attach yourself to the good part. When things start going in the bad direction, you start getting restless because your ego is attached to the good part. And I think this impulse to fragment everything into 2 comes from the impulse to associate philosophical meaning to things. If a Lion ate a Deer in the jungle, is it good or bad? Once you pick a side, you will always be opposed to the other side. This constant friction is what is the source of suffering. And so the sage advice is (pun intended) to let go. Let go of the good and the bad. Be one with everything. Once there is no fragmentation, there cannot be attachment to any particular thing since everything is equanimous. Without this attachment, there is no suffering. This however is most difficult to do at scale in all dimensions of life. We live in a dualistic world. Losing the idea of fragmentation would mean loosing your ego, your opinions, and then it becomes very difficult to collaborate in a world that operates on opinions.  

Meditatively: Breathe-in, breathe-out is how it starts and funnily enough that's how it ends too. The theory is that practicing the simplest of things brings your focus to now. And bringing focus to now will help you see the reality. The reality is just what is now, in front of you. Everything else about past and future is a thought. And with that practice, comes an ability to drop into the moment completely, without any distraction. This would enable you to focus on your action without worry. Because worry does not exist in reality. If it does, your mind already works on figuring it out and not thinking endlessly. Any thought which doesnt convert to any action or resolution in the now is a worry. When you start doing that, your mental conditioning changes to explore the moment fully and with that comes a ton of positive observations. This helps you live more holistically. You let the world come to you instead of controlling each facet of your life. The thing is, world is always providing you with chances, but in our attempts to control everything we are so occupied in our head that we are unable to pick those chances. Once we start living in the moment, we are easily able to grab everything that comes our way, and eventually it addresses all we wanted to achieve through control. It also makes life easy since you are equanimous to whatever comes your way and so the hard things become easy. All it requires is trusting the universe.  

What this does is, it makes you feel better about yourself and the world around you. You stop the constant judgment and negative self criticism. You start appreciating yourself more because now your focus is solving the problem in the now and enjoying it.
