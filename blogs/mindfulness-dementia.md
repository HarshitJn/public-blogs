# Mindfulness dementia

*Published: 2024-06-25*

---

And so the day comes, you are beat to the ground, not one but all ropes of reality fall apart. Insane amount of fear and restlessness pass through your body as you freely fall towards the ground. You succumb to the paranoia which makes you question your fabric of reality, thread by thread. Your simples rights become wrongs and nothing in you seems worthwhile. You find yourself at the crossroads of generating a false sense of control at one hand and a complete acceptance at the other. While the choice seems obvious, choosing the latter comes with a guilt of not doing enough and another fear of complacency is born.  
So what do you do? We all fail at what we are supposed to be. This is what Thor's mom said to the Nordic god of thunder. Sometimes all that's left to do is remove the lens of right and wrong, see the world for what it is and find beauty in it. So what if you have an IV pumping chemicals into your body while the likes of you are traveling to discover world. All experiences have their place in our lives and all have beauty to be found in them. At some places it is easy because they become a popular happiness destination. At others it is rather hard for the most aware souls to find it. It requires you to remove all the conditioning and fears born out of the conditioning. It requires you to stare at the face of the moment and just live through it by being one with it. As soon as you dissociate yourself from the moment and think about yourself in singularity, an unbounded weight of conscience drops on you. This doesn't just cloud your mind but also renders your cognition useless.  I have made worse off decision in adversity than I have in good times. And that's so counter intuitive but that's how our mind works. And you cant force yourself to start being positive in adversity if you stay in a infinite loop of right and wrong.  
Signing out, in hope, to live mindfully again.
