# Kabhi daakiye ko dekha nahi

*Published: 2025-03-28*

---

Maine kabhi daakiye ko dekha nahi  
Magar daakghar se guzarte hue lagta hai  
Sukoon hoga us waqt me.  
Ek kaagaz pe zehan ko bikher kar  
Kisi ke haathon apni takdeer saunp dete the log,  
"Doorstep pickup" ki zaroorat nahi padti hogi,  
Daak ghar tak ke raste me kadam roke nahi rukte honge.  
Mez pe rakhi siyaahi se dosti gehri lagti hogi,  
Jo pichli raat tumhare aansuon me ghul gayi thi.  
Woh kalam bhi yaad rehti hogi,  
Jisse pehli kavita phooti thi.  
Kuch lifaafon me phool zinda reh jate honge,  
Chitthiyon me behti hui prem kahani unhe saans deti hogi.  
Yaad jab satati hogi,  
Toh bakse se saari chitthiyan bikher kar  
Us ek par thehar jaate honge,  
Jiski raah me poora saavan guzar gaya tha.  
Aur shayad purani chitthiyan padhkar  
Un dino ka mausam laut aata hoga.  
Kaagaz me gade hue shabdon ko chhukar  
Uski ungliyon ka ehsaas jagta hoga.  
Kahin mez par jami dhool se  
Angoothe ki halki chhaap lifafe pe reh jaati hogi,  
Aur woh tumhari rekhaon me  
Khud ko talashati hogi.  
Sukoon hoga us waqt me.
