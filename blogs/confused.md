# Confused

*Published: 2025-03-05*

---

Something somewhere has changed. I dont want to make it big. Maybe this present moment was the dream always and now that I have it, I am lost. I dont want to make it bigger. While on the surface the solution just seems to reset and aim higher but I think there's more to it. During my way here, I found more love in going wider than higher. The easier thing with going higher is that you just keep putting more efforts in the same direction. You can get deeper and deeper. The harder thing about going wider is that you need to pick a direction. And that's difficult. Early 20s everyone is set to chase the same goals of money, work, health and the likes. But what then? What after? Poetry? Colors? Laughs? Peace? Love?
