# Won but Lost

*Published: 2023-09-10*

---

Story of a nagging kid  

The hero of our story  
A young kid, poor  
Imagine Charlie from the Chocolate Factory  
Like him he receives a one way ticket  
To a competition  
Only the stronger win  
He trips as he enters the premises  
Deception and lies horrify him  
Confused he rides the moral high  
And nags about the rich  
Hypocrites he calls them  

One day beaten up for his virtue  
He finds himself near a well of water  
The reflection makes him see  
His insufferable hypocricy  
Trying to get the upper hand  
By neglecting the truth  
The rules were simple  
The stronger one wins  

Resolved he gets up  
And plays it all  
His rights, he preaches  
Few pupils he gathers  
And his joy has no bounds  
Maybe it appeals to his identity  
Exercising the power validates his ego  
He strives for more  
And his charm rises  
Years go by  
He becomes a legend  
Now nowhere to be found  
A search party gets organized  
And his body floats in the same well of reflection  
He lost  
Not because he stopped playing  
But because he forgot he was playing.
