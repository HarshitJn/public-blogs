# Conscious Eating.

*Published: 2023-01-14*

---

1 heap full plate of poha, 2 bread slices with butter and a glass of chai. This is how I started most of my days during 2 years of lockdown. Dal, curd and 2 chapatis in lunch. A packet of chips/namkeen, kachori/samosa/sandwich, chaat here or there was a custom. Some biscuits. And a similar menu like lunch in the dinner followed by glass of chai before sleeping.  Here's a rough breakdown of this diet:Calories: 2200 cals  
Carbs: 240g  
Protein: 45g  
Fibre: 20g  
Fat: 90g  
This looks okay for the most part. A typical home meal. The issue comes when you do this over and over. There were no fruits in the diet, no seeds or nuts. It's low in protein, high in fat and carbs. And the fat here is mostly trans fat and saturated fat. There's hardly any good fat. Fibre content is low too.  
When I started working out about 6 months back, the importance of a good diet came naturally. I feel when you start putting efforts to burn calories, you start realising the damage you do to the body whole day. Otherwise the food industry, Instagram worthy cafes, no option but to eat out for the sake of doing something exciting will always get in the way. Food becomes a default comfort. And it should be so. Food releases dopamine. But a healthy diet could be comforting too. It's only difficult in the beginning, not because healthy food is not rewarding but because un-conditioning our habits is difficult.  
I have hated eating fruits all my life. They seemed to be too high an effort for no gain. Taste meant everything. I never cared to understand the basics of nutrition that were taught in elementary school. But slowly I turned that around. Here's what I did:  
I cut sugar from the diet. No sugar. For a month. I then slowly added fruits and started loving them. Rather I started craving the taste of an apple or a papaya. The reason was simple. I reset my brain to forget the sugar high and then fed it only natural sugar. And with fruits, came more water. I saw my water consumption doubling from around 2 litres to 4 litres per day. High fibre requires more water to be consumed. More water means high energy levels, sustained feeling of "fullness" and efficient loss of toxins to name a few. I also was fed up of the typical dal roti sabzi meal. Our brain has evolved to reward us more if we eat variety of foods. The dopamine release has found to be lower if the same food is consumed each day. This makes logical sense since consuming different foods is an efficient mechanism to get a balance of all the different vitamins and minerals. And so I started rotating my meals in the afternoon: added more legumes, a combination of seeds, sauteed vegetables, curd for the gut bacteria, removed chapati and added millets or brown rice etc. Solving protein was easy. Added a whey supplement post workout, a couple of glasses of no-fat milk, coconut water, seeds and legumes.  
I also committed a blunder. In efforts to get leaner, I removed all the fat from the diet. But not all fat is bad fat. I learnt this the hard way. Within one week of following a next to zero fat diet, I found my knees clicking and worse performance in the gym. I also had trouble focussing and increased fatigue during work hours. It's amazing how quickly body reacts to some of these changes. Later when I read about good fat bros MUFA and PUFA I understood how the omega fatty acids are crucial to brain function.  
I recently also added something that most moms would disapprove of. Supplements. Vitamin D 60k, once a week. Vitamin B12 Methylcobalamin 1500mcg, everyday. Fish oil 900mg EPA+DHA(PUFA), everyday.  
To compensate for my sins above I also added a glass of Amla juice and Tulsi to start my mornings and a glass of water/milk with ginger, turmeric, cardamon, cinnamon, cloves and pepper to end the day.  
Overall this is the list of food that I consume these days:Supplements: Vit B12 + Vit D + omega-3 + whey Digestion: Amla juice  
Immunity: Tulsi, ginger, turmeric, clove, pepper, cinnamon, cardamom, Kabasur Kudineer  
Breakfast: Coconut water, Upma, Masala Oats  
Lunch: Spinach, Dry vegetables, no curries, Dal, Brown Rice, Millet, Salad( Flax, Pumpkin, Sunflower seeds, Sprouts, Beans, Legumes, Sweet corn, Peas, Olives, Gherkins), Epigamia Natural Yogurt, Curd, Besan Cheela  
Snacks: Papaya, Orange, Apple, Ber, Strawberries, Banana, Carrot, Almonds, Walnut, Dark chocolate  
Dinner: Trim milk, Packaged Protein Muesli, Sattu, Soya chunks  
Its been only few months and I see myself less stressed than ever, high in energy, better sleep schedule, better quality of sleep, increased focus, increased drive to do hard things and overall happiness. Even though healthy eating started as a way to loose fat and keep track of calories I found that food is much more than calories. We are what we eat. And it shows up. Karke dekho, acha lagta hai
