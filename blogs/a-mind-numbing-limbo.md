# A mind numbing limbo

*Published: 2024-03-29*

---

I find myself at the crossroads of growth and restfulness. At one side the incessant need to climb mountains takes me away from the trees I planted of solace. At the other, doubt creeps in as I linger around the known gardens of peace. I feed my doubts and the restless lens morphs the comfort into complacency. My mind looses the grip and the seeds which became trees are long forgotten. I forget the days I spent waiting for the tree to grow in the scorching heat. The trees will soon be forgotten as I set forth on the climb. What happens when I reach up top and there’s nothing to see? Will I miss my trees now? Will I cherish the climb? Or would I descend the hill down to my garden of peace? What if the garden is now uprooted? Would I still cherish the climb?  
And so the deadweight hangs on my neck to materialise my identity. To create a tangible object. It doesn’t suffice to have felt or loved or understood or observed. The world wants you to prove it. You have to show it. And so what prevails is plagiarism. The intrinsic cant be measured and so has no value in the world which counts through eyes. And so starts the incessant struggle to create. Not to feel the process of creation but to be validated against the created. We live in a physical world and ironically that would be the end of reality. For the Reality can never be captured in objects.  
In this ear deafening conundrum to do or to be, all the beauty of the life fades way. I force myself to be gratuitous and very soon the illusive wall of self love tears apart. When you cannot force love another individual without them recognising, how can you force love yourself when you can see your brain rot away in its own cynicism. Where does this cynicism come from, I question myself. But my thoughts are muddled with what should be and not what is. The future past universe resides rent free in my brain and my capacity to process the present becomes subliminal. But then again, don’t we all live under the illusion of control of our lives, mapping our past to future with the supreme function of logic. It takes infinite courage to let the life happen to us and not go on controlling everything in sight. Milan Kundera, the Czech novelist was possibly observing the exact dichotomy when he said, “Ambition is a poor excuse for not having sense enough to be lazy”. And I couldn’t resonate more to anything so simple yet so true.  
Maybe the solution lies in going crazy with control. Maybe the solution demands extreme control at the cost of great suffering. But then maybe the solution is simply to be stupid. To not take life seriously. Sometimes I think the highest form of intelligence is perhaps great sadness or extreme stupidity, not anything in between.
