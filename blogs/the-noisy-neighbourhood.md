# The Noisy Neighbourhood

*Published: 2023-09-21*

---

All the mainstream measures are doomed to become targets. And so what prevails are silos of excellence with measures as abstract as clouds. I hope we don't forget the language these silos speak. I hope we don't stop looking at the cloud shapes we don't understand.  
As the world moves from being community centric to being very individual centric, we will soon lose all the language. And this by no means that we will lack the scripts or texts. If anything we are going to have an ever increasing number of languages, their authors and preachers. So much so that everyone would speak in a dialect that is so specific to their own truth that no one else can find it coherent. Humanity would suffer from the absence of meaning. When every concept, every idea is fragmented into pieces, what remains is mere rubble of non sense. And further what each party gets is a sheer share of shit. What we would see are these high and mighty words which would fail to have any impact since there is no shared context to understand their meaning.  And even within the people who use this shared language, the exchange of these words would be futile because they were not built in an attempt to convey meaning but as a weaponry to destroy meanings from those using a different language.  
And some of this is already starting to show. The woke half of the left has starting to preach its subjective truths in the guise of being liberal. This serves no one but the masters of that narrative, i.  
e those in powers, not the ones who are supposed to be liberalised by so called open speech. Lot of the equality narratives that float around today simply want the equality of outcome without any bearing on improving the overall wellbeing of the marginalised. Solving the problem at the root is hard, it takes digging into the nuances of each life and finding the common themes across and then finding ways which solve for the common themes. If we don't call bullshit on these, these movements inevitably become tools of those in power, for those in power by those already in power. And equality, like respect is a two way street. For example, being a man, standing for men doesn't make you misogynist, just like standing for women should not make you a compassionate narcissist. When we move away from objective truth, we end up mixing it all up.  
Where cheap laugh lines pollute the conversations  
And relative truth muddles up the conscious waters  
All word ends up becoming a fragment.  
When all movements are exploited  
And straw men fill up the narrative  
Viral trends shape up the masses  
Virtue signalling prevails  
And compassionate narcissism masquerades the lies  
Under a guise of benevolence.  
When all liberal thought gets confused for progress  
And all facts are disavowed  
And empathy is weaponised to perpetuate lies  
Any objective truth is projected as authoritarian.  
Then, once again the radical thought would win  
Not because it served the most  
Because the liberal thought  
Got polluted by the cowards.
