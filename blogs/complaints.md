# Complaints

*Published: 2025-02-17*

---

You say  
the magic of childhood  
has disappeared  
Have you recently stepped outside  
Saw that ball of fire  
It still shows up everyday  
Whole trees appear  
Out of just sand?  
Have you seen that?  
Do you realize  
The fruits you devour  
are just born out of water and dust.  
Have you loved another soul  
Just for a moment  
Without any fear or self interest?  
Have you seen the water  
Have you gone close to it  
And seen your reflection in it?  
Nature knows you, sees you.  
You think you see it,  
Forgetting its all one.  
The magic hasn't gone anywhere  
You've started spending  
Way too much time with  
These gadgets  
Forgetting that they were made by  
The people who never saw this magic.
