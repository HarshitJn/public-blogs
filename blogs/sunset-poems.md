# Sunset Poems

*Published: 2025-04-03*

---

Weather was nice today. Wrote a couple of short poems.
1
Sooraj poore din chaya rehta hai
Magar dhalte hue hi acha lagne lagta hai
Tum puchti ho itni der kyun kar di
Batlaunga, kal
Tum ye raat kaatne k liye
Bas ek chaand chod jana.
2
Daakghar, gram panchayat aur paan ki dukaan,
Sab kahaniyon mein simat gaye,
Magar chai ki tapri ab tak dhuaan utha rahi hai,
Waqt ke safhe palat kar bhi zinda hai.
Is be-tehasha daudti duniya mein,
Do pal dheema hone ka avsar mil jata hai.
Shukr hai, yeh bilkul muft nahi hai,
Kuch vyast aur chintit log
Ise fizool ka kaam kehkar mita chuke hote.
