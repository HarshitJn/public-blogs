# Sunset Poems

*Published: 2025-04-03*

---

Weather was nice today. Wrote a couple of short poems.
1Sooraj poore din chaya rehta haiMagar dhalte hue hi acha lagne lagta haiTum puchti ho itni der kyun kar diBatlaunga, kalTum ye raat kaatne k liyeBas ek chaand chod jana.
2Daakghar, gram panchayat aur paan ki dukaan,Sab kahaniyon mein simat gaye,Magar chai ki tapri ab tak dhuaan utha rahi hai,Waqt ke safhe palat kar bhi zinda hai.
Is be-tehasha daudti duniya mein,Do pal dheema hone ka avsar mil jata hai.
Shukr hai, yeh bilkul muft nahi hai,Kuch vyast aur chintit logIse fizool ka kaam kehkar mita chuke hote.
