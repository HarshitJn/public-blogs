# Winning Fat

*Published: 2023-01-09*

---

Visibility less than 10 meters. Nearing 0 degrees. Only a flimsy sweatshirt with college branding. It's 11 pm and as per the usual repertoire, we marched towards the all night canteen. The fashion challenged 20 year olds hardly understand whats wrong with socks and sandals, maybe we saw Mark Zuckerberg doing it(or was it Jesse Eisenberg). I digress. There was something in being in a hostel with 400 odd low maintenance blokes that made you strong beyond measure. There is an enigmatic strength in colonies of men. Or women.
Beyond fail we carried this schedule. It was a redemption of all the shit that was offered to us all day at the Sodexo mess. Cheese Sandwich was my standard, of course with a nonchalant request for extra cheese. Then there was white sauce pasta and the unbeatable masala dosa. In a world this complex, love had to be something so simple. Food was the answer.
Nothing pleasurable comes for free. I didn't know this back then. Although all these items cumulatively costed less than what 2 apples cost, I was paying with my health. As I passed my 6th semester, I had a tummy equivalent of a second trimester. It only got worse off from there. For years, I resorted to eating the same unhealthy food but with a known sense of guilt and a unkept promise of exercising soon in the future. This future came too late.
Later part of last year I determined to loose some belly fat. Some. I was not targeting abs in my first month but I borderline felt disgusted to carry on the unhealthy habits without so much as a hint of disconcertion. I started counting my calories using healthify. I found my basal metabolic rate and maintenance calories required to keep my weight intact using the calculators like this. I then added cardio to my regime. Nothing fancy. Just walking up the incline for 20 odd minutes twice a day. I calculated the calories lost per mental effort and found it to be highest for walking up the incline on a treadmill. It hardly needs any mental strength vs running or skipping rope. In addition to this I was already doing weight training in evenings so bundled up I started burning 600-700 cals per day. Everyday including most weekends. I lost 5 kgs in a month. Easily. I never checked my weight before the month end, I knew I felt good after the workout so that was enough. I remember what the good old man Goodhart said, "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure". Applies everywhere. 
I still have long way to go to get a flat tummy or some abs but I don't shy away from sharing this before that happens. I am confident that it's not difficult. All it needs is our conviction and our minds will find a way to do it. Here's another taboo I want to break from this blog. Jinxing growth. We seldom share our progress stories while they are happening lest we should jinx them. There's so much pseudo science around "not sharing your goals before you achieve them" cuz your brain gets happy with the fake "dopamine" and then you don't feel like working on your goal, yada yada. I find it to be so much bullshit. How can anything good happen when all the while you live in the fear of losing it because of such trivialities. We are raising ourselves to be fragile. To live in fear. We should aim at becoming self aware to the point where sharing our progress only motivates us to do better, to put in more efforts. There's nothing enlightened about shrinking so that other people won’t feel insecure around you. And, as we let our own light shine, we consciously give other people permission to do the same. As we are liberated from our fear, our presence automatically liberates others. Some of you might remember this scene from Coach Carter.
Being humble and hiding behind the masks of mediocracy are not the same. I think when we start promoting ourselves for the things we are good at, we also start becoming more open to accepting things we suck at. So cheers to everyone who made some resolutions this year, may we all get better at things we love. May we all get stronger. May we all get the medals we incessantly chase. Most importantly, may we all feel happy, while we're on the way.
samastha lokah sukhino bhavantu.
