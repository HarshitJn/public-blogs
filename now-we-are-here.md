# Now we are here.

*Published: 2023-09-14*

---

The banal morality and compassionate narcissism is the real death of pursuit of wonder. In order to be able to seek truth, you have to risk offending someone. It also needs an ability to question the roots of judgement both at an individual level and at society at large. This pursuit has to be built on the principle of net positive and not self righteousness. At the same time it needs to avoid condemnation and follow a path of judgement free observation. Condemnation does not liberate, it only oppresses. The only path to changing anything is accepting it, totally, completely. Most of these efforts would come across as tyrannical to the surface thinkers. Do not fall prey to their coddling. Theres nothing enlightening about trivialising the truth, if anything it is a crime to do so. Your playing small does not serve the world. The answers that you're looking for are not going to be found in what you already know. Availability bias is a curse and needs to be uprooted so deeply that what fills that hole is an incessant thirst for knowledge. This not only takes you closer to the truth but also increases your authority and competence, which is what creates a metallic fabric of power. Power that does not corrupt but expands you. Power that does not frighten others but liberates them from fear. Power that others can lean onto to grow and then walk alone. And maybe in this process you will stumble upon beauty, and it will make you smile and make all your pain worthwhile. There will be an inevitable tussle between identity and stateless beauty. Through this contradiction, you will see clearly what this is all about.
