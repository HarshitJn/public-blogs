# The last village

*Published: 2024-07-21*

---

Far from the urban jungle, there is still a world which is unadulterated with the follies of the green cotton. There exists a world where you can stay in public and still be at peace. You dont need to have all your guards up. The mouths dont try to outsmart each other. You are not always on the edge of an exploit. Money is still valued here. It has not lost its value as compared to those far western towns where the infinite supply has rendered each unit worthless. The exchange of money happens with a certain degree of respect. The feelings of fraternity and community are still present, however bleak. Whoever are the bearers of this village, tell them that its on them to protect this world from going extinct. On them lies the great burden of humanity.
