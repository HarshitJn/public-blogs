# Boondo ka lifafa

*Published: 2024-12-22*

---

Khafa tha samandar seToh ek chithi likhiEk nazm aur kuch sangeeda shabd
Ek nadi ke tat pe jaakeBharose se use saunp diyaSamandar se khrodit tha na mai
Lifafe me samete hue shabdKuch der nadi ke saathkhilkhilate hue behte gaye
Na jaane kyun nadi apne astitv ko samundar me baha deti haiKitne vipreet hain yeMaaf karein mai thoda bhatak gaya
Meri Chitthi samandar tak pohonchiAur mere shabdo ki syaahi failne lagiEk ek karke saare shabd doob gaye
Mai samjha ki yahi reet hogiSamandar me doobke hi use samjha ja skta hogaShayad samandar meri chitthi ka jawab dega.
Kayi dino se barsaat nhi huiAbki baar boondo ka maane samjhunga
