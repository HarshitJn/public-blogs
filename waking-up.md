# Waking up

*Published: 2025-04-17*

---

There's really just one way to live - by having a drive, a direction, a passion, anger, a mission to be/do/achieve something. That something , more often than not is overcoming the current problems life has thrown at you. And in the process if you be a little creative you might even laugh along the way. Modern liberal society with all its low effort distractions falsely marketed as freedom and fun will make you believe that whiling away your time is good enough. Only that it doesn't work. Because its only half the story. Rest and fun and freedom is great only when you are busy chasing something great. The absurdity of human life and the challenges that come our way are impossible to deal with unless you have a solid foundation of your identity. Who you want to be helps you navigate all the uncertainty and complex situations. And this is where most philosophers go wrong. JK for example spent his life people to leave all the fragmentation behind and not think in terms or right and wrong. The idea is liberating and can provide the freedom from the burden of trying to be on the right side. It however fails miserably because humans get a drive to do something only when they think its better than its opposite. We think in contrast and thats what drives us. Its ironical that the chase will make you feel miserable but so will the absence of it. The answer is in the balance. Yin and the yang. The answer is to become a Peaceful Warrior. To be in a meditative chase. To know that the result doesnt matter but its the effort makes you feel alive. Knowing that there is nothing better to do than finding something worthwhile to take your life away.
