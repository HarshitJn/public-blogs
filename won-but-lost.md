*Published: 2023-09-10*

Story of a nagging kid
The hero of our storyA young kid, poorImagine Charlie from the Chocolate FactoryLike him he receives a one way ticketTo a competitionOnly the stronger winHe trips as he enters the premisesDeception and lies horrify himConfused he rides the moral highAnd nags about the richHypocrites he calls them
One day beaten up for his virtueHe finds himself near a well of waterThe reflection makes him seeHis insufferable hypocricyTrying to get the upper handBy neglecting the truthThe rules were simpleThe stronger one wins
Resolved he gets upAnd plays it allHis rights, he preachesFew pupils he gathersAnd his joy has no boundsMaybe it appeals to his identityExercising the power validates his egoHe strives for moreAnd his charm risesYears go byHe becomes a legendNow nowhere to be foundA search party gets organizedAnd his body floats in the same well of reflectionHe lostNot because he stopped playingBut because he forgot he was playing.
