# Inertia of Activity

*Published: 2023-10-22*

---

An object at rest remains at rest, and an object in motion remains in motion at constant speed and in a straight line unless acted on by an unbalanced force.
No I'm not revisiting elementary physics to find the time it takes for the housefly before it gets squashed between the trains. Instead Im amazed to see how the rudimentary principles of physics apply to human behaviour as well.  I call it Inertia of activity. I think we all have had huge plans for our summer vacations, all made during the yearly exams. And what happens when we actually are in those vacations. Nothing. It goes out like a long long afternoon slump. We often make plans during high state of moving energy and aren't able to execute it anywhere close.
But I digress. I wanted to visit this is another light. That is, to create an ability to make this switch between motion and rest at will. I observed something interesting at work. I have a particularly intense and focused style of working that continues throughout the day until I want to get up and get some evening tea. And thats where I observed that how difficult it is to fight that inertia of motion. I keep delaying making that switch from high speed activity to slowing down, observing the surroundings and socialize a bit. If I dont do it consciously, I end up returning home with a running mind which has a list of things pending in its head to finish off the next day. And I miss out on having fun. This model gives a short term adrenaline and creates an aura of super efficiency but does not scale in long term. Human mind creates magic in the moments of silence. If I dont give myself those moments where it wanders without an agenda, I would never be able to have a non-linear growth. People who grow non linearly dont look stressed. Because they aren't. And I feel this equation of shifting the momentum at will plays a huge part in it. It applies to both directions but I feel there is a lot of literature and thought around going into a state of movement from inactivity. David Goggins and Jordan Petersons of the world will keep pushing the lazy ones to become go-getters. But not enough is spoken about going from movement to silence. And in the world where each minute of silence is competed by 6 10 second short videos on TikTok, it becomes even more imperative.
The how is pretty straightforward. You need to let go. Be okay with embracing the slow. Be okay with slipping on other lucrative things. Take a leap of faith and witness the amazing things awaiting you on the slower side. Just remember Newton and break that inertia.
