# Love and other hard things

*Published: 2024-08-06*

---

When you are surrounded by love, with people who have nothing but good thoughts for you and for life, all the trivialities of not having enough in life become infinitesimal. You feel at rest, psychologically, and that rest then can be converted to lot of movement. Doing hard things become easy. Probably just because you feel more self-assured and assured about life. Its funny how they say that if you want to walk far, you must walk alone. I now find that all the seeming operational inefficiencies occurring due to emotional bandwidth spent, gives you more than you spend. Just spend time with people who love you and their own life. 
Now, then it doesn't mean that it can come easy. More than the others, it will require you to see goodness in others and find creative ways to resolve conflicts. Instead of pursuing relationships with self-righteousness approaching them through wonder and curiosity will make lives easy.
I think a lot of sourness in relationships comes from our own self-loathing. We constantly fear our lives in terms of what should have been (present) and what could be (future). If we start seeing life more holistically, we will find our fears irrational and quite futile. Comparison is the thief of joy. What helps is to focus on the important things - family and health. Other superfluous things like travelling or a huge bank balance shouldn't become the reason of unhappiness. 
Here's a thought experiment: Whenever you feel that you are incomplete in this moment, because you are not where you could have been right now or because you will not be able to get to where you wanted to be in future, think about a moment 5-10 years ago. Weren't you wishing for the exact life that you have now? More or less? Isnt this all you ever wanted? Hasnt the universe conspired to get you here against all odds? If so, why dont you just surrender this fear of not being enough? 
Here's my twist on the Henry Ford quote: Whether you think you are enough or you think you're not, you're right. That is to say life will appear to you the way you see it. That is not to say, you waste your life sitting like a hobo. Do the things that make you happy. Do the hard things. Dont fear the ambiguity. Embrace it and you will see that all your relationships with people will get better.
